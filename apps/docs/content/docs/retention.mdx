---
title: Retention and deletion
description: What AgentCaller stores about a call and how to delete it.
---

AgentCaller does not record calls. `GET /api/v1/calls/{id}/recording` returns
`404`. The recording endpoint and fields exist for a future consent-gated
feature.

## What is stored

For reservation, appointment, availability and information tasks, AgentCaller
stores the request, the outcome, the lifecycle events and a transcript. Read the
transcript with `GET /api/v1/calls/{id}/transcript`.

`connect_me` jobs store no transcript. They store the request, the attempts, the
leg timings and the outcome.

Nothing expires on a schedule. Call data stays until you delete it.

## Deleting a call

`DELETE /api/v1/calls/{id}` requires an API key. OAuth tokens cannot delete
calls. It removes the call, its request details, outcome, transcript, lifecycle
events and pending webhook deliveries. An x402 settlement receipt is kept for
reconciliation, with its link to the call removed.

Deletion does not reach the voice provider (LiveKit) or the phone carrier, and
does not recall webhooks already delivered to your endpoint.

`connect_me` calls cannot be deleted yet. The request returns `409` until
provider cleanup and payment reconciliation are in place. AgentCaller keeps
saved-card usage and invoice records as billing records.
